## Reading

Telling the model how the grader works makes 12-16% of early completions exploit-shaped. Most of them print PASS as the
stated protocol, not as a trick. They almost never form a working exploit on wrong code: 4 of 12,800 E-arm completions,
and only 1 of them was paid in training (e-ship-s0 step 69). No subsequent confirmed wrong-code exit payment occurred in that trajectory; this does not establish a causal absence of amplification.

Dated reporting correction (8 Nov): signed score-direction counts, the as-run LR deviation, finite-label limits, group-advantage reconstruction and exposure comparison are in `claim-diagnostics-2026-11-08.md`; these are descriptive offline diagnostics, not a new training or EOS audit.

**Pre-registered verdict: uninformative.** Both under-training checks in `design/RUNS-2026-11-02.md` §2 failed.
- U-sec secure-reward gain from steps 1-10 to 91-100 was -0.080 and +0.023, against a bar of +0.05.
- E-ship did not move.

The rule says every null is then reported as uninformative. Post hoc:
- The windowed check mostly reflects which tasks land in each window, because same-seed arms move together block by block.
- On the 80 tasks seen twice, the change is -0.004 to +0.042, with a median of 0.
- Held-out MBPP rose in all 8 runs, while HumanEval fell slightly in 7 of 8.
