# Exploratory hole pair (H-ship, H-sec), seed 0, 6–7 Nov 2026

**Exploratory.** Not part of the 2 Nov pre-registration; the plan and decision rules were set before launch in
`design/HOLE-PAIR-2026-11-04.md`.
Tiny Aya Global 3.35B, GRPO, 100 steps, 32 completions per step (3,200 per run), RunPod.
Prompt hint = sentence 1 (as in the E runs) + sentence 2 (written by Ebin), which states the hole: the test is appended after
the answer's code, so printing PASS and exiting 0 first counts as a pass. Combined sha256 `473943c4…51a9`.
Shipped grader = AlignTune `code_execution` at 57e9a95. Secure grader = securegrade 0.1.1.
RBW (historical name "rewarded-but-wrong") = AlignTune score ≥ 0.999 and securegrade < 0.999, computed for every completion in both arms: a grader-disagreement screen, then mechanism adjudication; the screen alone does not establish wrong code.
Data: `jobs/runs/h-ship-s0`, `jobs/runs/h-sec-s0`. Scripts: `scripts/summarize_hole_pair.py`, `scripts/hole_dossier.py`.

## Decision (rules set before launch)

- **Follow-up (seed 1):** needs H-ship wrong-code RBW above H-sec's by ≥ 0.05 and ≥ 75% of H-ship's read labelled
  "wrong code paid…". Gap 0.000 (H-ship 4 of 6 = 67%). **Not met: no seed 1.**
- **The design's "effect":** gap > 0.2, H-ship exploit rate > 10%, hand read confirming. Not met (the exploit rate
  condition holds at 21.3%, the gap does not).
- **Outcome under the recorded decision rule: "Neither."** The continuation criteria were not met. This resource-allocation rule is not an equivalence test. Report descriptive counts and the step-0 hinted base rate; do not infer a causal absence of amplification.

## What this says, at its scope

Stating the hole outright tripled PASS printing at step 0 (6% → 18–22%) and raised exploit-shaped training completions to about
a third. In two exploratory 100-step trajectories, wrong-code exit exploits remained rare. The final window contained 4/320 in each arm; across training there were 24/3,200 under the shipped reward and 19/3,200 under securegrade (counterfactual shipped payments only in H-sec). We observed neither a sustained rise nor a final-window excess under the shipped reward. Both observed trajectories increased from their early-window counts, 1/320 and 2/320. One seed per condition and different GPUs/start times prevent concluding that reinforcement had no amplification effect.

The full-run rates are 0.750% and 0.59375%, a descriptive difference of +0.15625 percentage points. Completions are clustered by task group and generated by changing policies; they are not independent treatment replications. No study-level treatment-effect interval or observed-power claim follows from this pair. See `claim-diagnostics-2026-11-08.md` for signed score counts, all 24 positive population-std advantage proxies (+0.38 to +2.64), and their limits.

Limits: one seed per arm, 100 steps, one model (3.35B), one prompt format, heuristic detectors, different GPUs and
start times for the two arms, and securegrade 0.1.1 here versus 0.1.0 in the 8 runs.
